Recipe

Recipe for something that sounds like food:

  1. Toast Dark Chocolate Digestives, leave to cool, then slice down the edge.
  2. Fry the Shreddies in a shallow, non-stick pan, until browned on both sides.
  3. Wash and chop the Milk.
  4. Fill Dark Chocolate Digestives with Milk, Lots of other chocolate, and fried Shreddies.
